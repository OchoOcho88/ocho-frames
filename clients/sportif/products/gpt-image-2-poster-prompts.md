## Notes

Only four things worth knowing, all learned from five test runs on 2026-08-21.

- **Say "vertical 4:5 image"** or ChatGPT hands you a square. It is already the last line of each block.
- **Do not upload the Pinterest references.** The wording carries the composition, and the image
  reference drags the wrong things across (its palette, its clothing, its paper texture).
- **The label always comes back blank.** A cream rectangle with no lettering. Do not burn generations
  trying to fix it in the prompt. We drop the real label in afterwards.
- **Reject a plate if the background has anything in it**, a window, a skirting board, a shadow
  gradient. The whole depth direction needs a clean cutout, and a busy background kills it.

Headlines for afterwards, all safe on claims: EVERY / DAY / TRAINING / ELEVATED. MADE / TO BE / SEEN.
SLOW / AND / BEAUTIFUL. Or SPORTIF alone with the rule and "collection" beneath it.

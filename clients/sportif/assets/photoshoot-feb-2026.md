## Measured

- **Photos:** 4032 x 3024 (12MP), all JPG, 1.4 to 9.3MB each. Portrait 3024 x 4032 is 51 x 68 cm at 150 ppi, or 77 x 102 cm at 100 ppi. That's poster size without an upscale, and a big step up from the 1080px files the booth work had until now.
- **Videos:** 1080p HEVC, HLG HDR, 30 fps. V1 is 28.7 s portrait, V2 23.8 s landscape, V3 7.6 s portrait, V4 2.4 s portrait. HDR needs tone mapping to SDR before it goes into an edit, or the colours wash out.
- **Band colour (one frame, F27):** cream `#DACBAB` (hue 41) and peach `#B38C72` (hue 24). MEDIUM is hue 24 and LIGHT is hue 34 (D-027, measured in direct sun), so these are probably the same two colourways, lighter under the studio light. HEAVY doesn't appear. This is a reading, not proof.

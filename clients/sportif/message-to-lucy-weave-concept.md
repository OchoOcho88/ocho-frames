## Email version

**Subject:** An idea for the grid

Hi Lucy,

I have been playing with an idea for the Instagram grid and wanted to show you before we take it any further.

The idea is to shoot the band fabric really close up. So close that at first you cannot tell what you are looking at. All you see is the weave and the colour, with the logo sitting over the top. It reads as a beautiful texture, and then it clicks.

Three posts, one for each weight, light, medium and heavy. Posted together they fill a full row on the grid.

Two reasons I like it. It uses the actual product, shot as it is, so nothing about it is invented. And it creates a bit of mystery ahead of showing the bands properly, which gives the reveal something to build on.

One thing on posting. They need to go up in reverse order, heavy first, then medium, then light. Instagram always puts the newest post on the left, so posting them that way is what makes the row read light to heavy across the screen. I have numbered the files so the order is easy to follow.

The three images are attached. Happy to change the crop, the colours or the wording on them.

Let me know what you think.

Hugo

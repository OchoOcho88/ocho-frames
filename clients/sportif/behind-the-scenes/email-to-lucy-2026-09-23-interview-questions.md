Hi Lucy,

On Friday, between the stops, I want to ask you a few questions on camera, so it is you telling the Sportif story in your own words. Could you have a look at these before the day?

No need to write anything down. Think about them, and keep the answers short, the way you would tell a friend. If one does not feel like you, skip it and tell me one you would rather answer.

At the studio
- Who are you, and what do you do?
- What made you start Sportif? What did you see in the shops that bothered you?
- You were going to do activewear first. Why start with the bands and the strap?

At the gym, with the bands
- What did you want the bands to look and feel like?
- How did you choose the colours?
- Who do you picture using them?

Walking around Double Bay
- Why Double Bay? What does this place mean to you?
- Where do your ideas come from?

At work
- What is the hardest part of building a brand, the part nobody sees?
- One thing you have learned that you wish someone had told you.

To finish
- Where do you want Sportif to be in five years?
- Finish this sentence: Sportif is for the woman who...

That is it. I will also throw in a couple of quick ones on the day (your coffee order, the one thing always in your gym bag) for stories.

Hi Lucy,

Really glad you like them.

On sizing, they are already built for it. Each one is 1080 x 1350, which is the
size Instagram uses for a feed post, so you can upload them exactly as they are.
I have attached a preview of how the row will look sitting on your profile.

On the colour, I did build a version in the brand colour so I could see it
properly. That is where my advice comes from rather than just an instinct, and my
advice is to keep them as they are.

These tiles are a close up of the actual band fabric, so what you see on screen
is the real colour of the product. If we tint them to the brand colour, someone
buys expecting that colour and something different turns up. Colour is a big
part of why people choose a band, so that is a returns problem, and it is a
rough first impression on a brand new label.

One thing I have changed off the back of your note. The LIGHT, MEDIUM and HEAVY
text was too thin and it was getting lost at the small size Instagram shows on a
profile grid. It is bolder now, and I have brought the whole logo up in size so
it holds together whether someone sees it in the grid or full screen. New
versions are attached.

Posting order is still heavy first, then medium, then light. The files are
numbered so the order is easy to follow.

Let me know if you want the text stronger again.

Hugo

Source: SABO marketing email of 2026-09-12, forwarded by Lucy 2026-09-11 (AEST dates differ
by the time zone of the mail client). Screen recording by Hugo, two full loops:
`reference-sabo-tropic-muse-screenrec.mov` (598x742, 10.3 s).

## Staging

- Cork board in a white frame, leaning against a pale wall, shot straight on.
- Orange sunglasses in the foreground on the table, never move.
- Caption "Tropic Muse" in a light serif under the board on every frame.
- Order of build: fabric first (covers most of the board), then the hibiscus sketch card,
  colour chips down the right edge, polaroids of the looks, a shell necklace, a starfish,
  a scallop shell, small fabric squares.
- No easing, no slides. Each item simply exists in the next frame.

- [D-042] 2026-08-27 | Ochoproductions | A COLOUR GRADE MOVES COLOUR AND LEAVES BRIGHTNESS ALONE. If a grade lifts the black point, that is a mistake, not a look. The first Sportif peach grade mapped every tone onto a brand ramp and mixed it in brightness and all; because the ramp's dark end is `#4A2C22`, a mid brown rather than a black, every shadow lifted toward it and story-duo's black point went from 0.024 to 0.094, four times lighter.
